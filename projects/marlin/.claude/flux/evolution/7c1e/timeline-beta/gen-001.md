# Timeline-beta, gen-001

## Approach

CSA: all connections in one array sorted by departure time. Scan forward from the start
time, keep an earliest-arrival array over stops, stop at start + max travel time.

## Why this approach could win

- trivially simple inner loop, a single pass over a time window
- the time horizon cuts the scan directly: only connections in [start, start+max] matter
- max wait time and allowed modes are plain per-connection predicates, very cheap

## Risks

- max transfers needs an extra per-stop counter, which weakens the simple earliest-arrival
  invariant (a later arrival with fewer transfers can be better)
- a 120 min window on the whole SL network may still be a large scan
